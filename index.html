3. 地圖：三條路徑與濃度場3. The map: three paths and the concentration field

地圖 6000 × 6000，左下是我方晶都，右上是敵方熔爐要塞。三條路的地面圖案直接畫出擴散機制：The 6000 × 6000 map has your citadel at lower left and the Furnace fortress at upper right. Each path's floor shows its mechanism:

  • 晶界捷徑（上路）Grain-boundary path (top)多晶晶粒與發光晶界，D 最大（×4）。Polycrystal grains with glowing boundaries; largest D (×4).
  • 空位之路（中路）Vacancy path (mid)規則原子排列中散布紫色空位，D ×1.6。A regular lattice dotted with violet vacancies; D ×1.6.
  • 間隙之路（下路）Interstitial path (bottom)大原子之間的綠色小原子在間隙中，D ×2.6。Small green atoms in the gaps between large atoms; D ×2.6.
  • 等溫河與野區Isotherm river & jungle河道 D 很小，像擴散屏障；野區 D 較小。The river has a tiny D and acts as a barrier; the jungle has a smaller D.

地面的藍色／紅色就是溶質濃度 C(x,y,t)。每座防禦塔與主堡都是固定表面濃度 Cs 的「源」，原子兵走過會留下溶質，技能也能注入溶質或升溫。細線是等濃度線（每 10%），亮白線是擴散前緣（C = 50%），橘色光暈是升溫區。濃度場以 192 × 192 網格（每格約 31 單位）由 Fick 第二定律在背景執行緒（Web Worker）即時計算；選「硬體加速」時還會用 WebGPU 運算。The blue/red tint is the solute concentration C(x,y,t). Every tower and core is a source held at a fixed surface concentration Cs; atomlings deposit solute as they march, and skills inject solute or heat. Thin lines are iso-concentration contours (every 10%), the bright line is the diffusion front (C = 50%) and orange glow marks heated ground. Fick’s second law is solved live on a 192 × 192 grid (about 31 units per cell) in a background thread (Web Worker), on WebGPU compute when Hardware is selected.

「擴散 (diffusion)」最精確的定義是？Which best defines diffusion?
  1. 液體因重力流動Liquid flowing under gravity
  2. 物質藉由原子運動而進行的質量傳輸Mass transport by atomic motion
  3. 電子在導體中的移動Electron flow in a conductor
  4. 晶粒在加熱時長大Grain growth on heating
擴散是藉由原子運動造成的質量傳輸，在固體中也會發生。Diffusion is mass transport by atomic motion, and it happens in solids too.
把銅塊與鎳塊緊密接合後長時間加熱，兩種原子互相跨越界面擴散，這稱為？A Cu block and a Ni block are joined and heated for a long time; atoms of each cross the interface. This is called:
  1. 自擴散Self-diffusion
  2. 再結晶Recrystallization
  3. 互擴散（雜質擴散）Interdiffusion (impurity diffusion)
  4. 析出硬化Precipitation hardening
兩種金屬原子由高濃度往低濃度互相擴散，稱為互擴散。Atoms of two metals move from high to low concentration into each other: interdiffusion.
純金屬中，同種原子彼此交換位置的擴散稱為？In a pure metal, diffusion of host atoms among themselves is called:
  1. 自擴散Self-diffusion
  2. 互擴散Interdiffusion
  3. 間隙擴散Interstitial diffusion
  4. 穩態擴散Steady-state diffusion
純金屬中同種原子的遷移稱為自擴散，因成分不變，常以放射性同位素追蹤。Host atoms migrating in a pure metal is self-diffusion; composition doesn't change, so radioactive tracers are used to follow it.
原子要藉空位機制跳到鄰位，必須具備哪兩個條件？For an atom to move by the vacancy mechanism, which two conditions are needed?
  1. 外加電場與磁場An electric and a magnetic field
  2. 高壓與液態High pressure and a liquid state
  3. 晶界與差排A grain boundary and a dislocation
  4. 鄰近有空位，且原子有足夠能量越過能障An adjacent empty site and enough energy to break bonds and squeeze past
兩個條件：(1) 相鄰位置是空的；(2) 原子有足夠的振動能量打斷鍵結、擠過鄰原子。Two conditions: (1) an empty adjacent site; (2) enough vibrational energy to break bonds and distort the lattice while moving.
在多數金屬中，間隙擴散比空位擴散快得多，主要原因是？Interstitial diffusion is usually much faster than vacancy diffusion because:
  1. 間隙原子比較重Interstitial atoms are heavier
  2. 間隙原子較小、可移動的鄰近間隙位置遠多於空位Interstitial atoms are smaller and empty interstitial sites far outnumber vacancies
  3. 間隙擴散不需要能量Interstitial diffusion needs no energy
  4. 間隙原子帶電Interstitial atoms are charged
小原子活動性高，而且鄰近的空間隙位置比空位多得多，所以跳躍機率高。Small atoms are more mobile, and there are many more empty interstitial positions than vacancies.
